Connect your WhatsApp number to Symmetrybot

Connect your WhatsApp Business account to Symmetrybot. Our team sets up an AI assistant with your business information to answer customer questions. You choose the account and number to connect.

What happens when you connect

  1. Sign in with Facebook and choose your business.
  2. Pick or create your WhatsApp Business account and phone number.
  3. Complete the verification steps Meta shows you, then return here to confirm your account and number.

You choose which WhatsApp assets Symmetrybot can access. We use the authorized account information to verify your connection and prepare your messaging service. Facebook handles your sign-in.

Use the second option to keep using an eligible number in the WhatsApp Business app. Connecting your account does not turn on automated replies: our team completes your assistant setup first.
