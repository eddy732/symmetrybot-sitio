Connect your WhatsApp number to Symmetrybot

Symmetrybot sets up an AI assistant that answers your customers on WhatsApp: it replies to questions, takes orders and notifies you of new ones. Sign in with Facebook to link your WhatsApp Business account. You choose which number to connect.

What happens when you connect

  1. Sign in with Facebook and choose your business.
  2. Pick or create your WhatsApp Business account and phone number.
  3. Verify the number with the code WhatsApp sends you.

Symmetrybot only receives the access needed to send and receive messages and to manage message templates for the account you connect. Facebook handles your sign-in. This page does not request or store your Facebook profile name.

The second option uses Meta’s onboarding for eligible numbers in the WhatsApp Business app. Automated replies require separate server activation.
